## Key findings at a glance
- Korea's exports in HS 1704/1806/1905/2105 rose **US$543M → US$789M (2020–25)**. **Volume was flat**; only **ice cream** grew in real terms (+13.1%/yr).
- The USA, Japan and China take **57%** of these exports, a concentration risk.
- Chocolate import value rose **+21.7%** in 2025 while volume fell **−13.3%** (cocoa shock). Growth must be measured in volume.
- A naïve screen puts the **Netherlands #3** (re-export hub); corrected, it drops out.
- Preliminary corrected TOP3: **USA, China, Philippines**. The third slot is weight-sensitive (Canada, India and Malaysia are close), so the product must show **P(TOP3)**.
- Lotte context: overseas share ~28.6% (target 35% by 2028); Pepero is the global mega brand (SEA and North America); local production in India and Kazakhstan; China manufacturing exit; 15% US tariff since Aug 2025.
