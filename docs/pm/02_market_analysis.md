# 02 — Market Analysis

> **Question:** Where should Lotte Wellfood push Korean-made confectionery, bakery and ice cream exports next, and what does a decision tool need to know to answer that well?
>
> **Inputs:** Desk research on 2026-10-29 (sources listed at the end; figures marked *unverified* where sources disagree or are secondary) and our own trade-data analysis ([03_data_analysis.md](03_data_analysis.md)).

---

## 1. Executive summary
1. **K-food demand is real and still growing, but unevenly.**
   - Korea's agri-food exports passed **US$10.4B in 2025** (+4.3%). Biscuits & confectionery reached **US$770M in 2024 (+17.4%)**, and ice cream passed **US$100M in 2025 (+21.6%)** [S1, S2, S6].
   - Our volume analysis shows much of the category growth is **price, not volume**. Ice cream is the clear exception.
2. **Lotte Wellfood is under-internationalised versus peers.**
   - About **28.6%** of revenue comes from overseas (exports ₩239.6B + subsidiaries ₩965.1B in 2025), against **~66% at Orion** [L2, C1].
   - Management targets **35% overseas by 2028** and has named **Pepero** the group's first **global mega brand**, prioritising **Southeast Asia and North America** [L3, L4].
3. **Lotte's footprint changes what "export market" means.**
   - It produces locally in **India** (Lotte India + Havmor, merged 2025), **Kazakhstan** (Rakhat), **Russia**, **Pakistan**, **Myanmar** and **Belgium** (Guylian), and is **exiting manufacturing in China** [L1, L5].
   - A tool that ranks only "exports from Korea" will misjudge these markets, so we need an **entry-mode lens**.
4. **Policy shocks are frequent and material.**
   - A **15% US tariff** has applied to Korean goods since August 2025 [R1].
   - **Indonesia** moved mandatory halal certification for imported food from Oct 2026 to **18 Oct 2028** (PP 40/2026, enacted 14 Oct 2026) [R5].
   - Cocoa hit **>US$12,000/t** in late 2024 and was ~US$6,200/t in Dec 2025 [M1].
   - **Monitoring matters as much as the initial ranking.**
5. **Preliminary hypothesis for TOP3, to be confirmed by the product:**
   - **USA:** scale and momentum, tariff headwind.
   - **Philippines:** fastest-growing Korean export market among large destinations, named Pepero priority region, #2 ice-cream market in some periods.
   - **A contested third slot:** China (size, but volume decline and Lotte's manufacturing exit), Canada (stable, Costco traction), or Malaysia/India (fast real growth). The product's job is to make this trade-off transparent.

### 3.3 Export product portfolio
- **Pepero:**
  - Exports rose from **₩29B (2020) to ₩87B (2025)**, about 3× [L3]. 2024 export sales were ₩70.1B (+~30%) [L2].
  - Top markets: North America, Southeast Asia (notably the Philippines), Russia, Saudi Arabia, Mongolia.
  - Target: a top-10 global snack brand and #1 in Asia by 2035 [L4].
- **Choco Pie:** ₩14.0B in export sales (2024) [L2].
- **ZERO (sugar-free):** exported to 13 countries. H1 2025 exports grew +246% [L5].
- **Ice cream:** World Cone (exported to 14 countries), Screw Bar and Jaws Bar. Lotte ice cream exports "more than doubled" 2021→2025 [L10, S7]. *Melona is Binggrae, not Lotte.*

**Category fit with the data:**
- Pepero is mostly **HS 1905** (biscuit sticks), so it is cocoa-exposed but sits outside HS 1806.
- **Ice cream (HS 2105)** is the only category with strong real volume growth (+13.1%/yr).
- Chocolate (1806) is in a volume decline driven by the cocoa price shock.

→ **Category filters are essential:** the TOP3 for ice cream may differ from the TOP3 for biscuits.

## 7. Macro risks
- **Cocoa:** from a historical US$2–3k/t to **>US$12k/t (late 2024)**, then ~US$6.2k/t by Dec 2025, still about 2× the norm [M1]. This hits Pepero and Ghana, which are about 30% of Lotte's confectionery sales [M2]. In the trade data, chocolate import value rose +21.7% in 2025 while volume fell −13.3%.
- **FX:** a weak won makes exports more price-competitive but raises imported raw-material costs. The weak yen hurts exports to Japan [S1, C1].
- **Tariffs:** the US tariff falls hardest on firms without a US plant, which includes Lotte [L11].
- **Concentration:** the USA, Japan and China take 57% of Korea's category exports ([03 F2](03_data_analysis.md)).
- **Freight costs:** *unverified*, so the product should not rely on them.
